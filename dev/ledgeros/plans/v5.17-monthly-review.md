# v5.17.0 plan: Monthly review (#68)

Status: answered 2026-11-14 (R1 both, R2 median, R3 OK, R4 overwrite with old vs new month by month, R5 third tab + to-do) and shipped as v5.17.0 the same day. Invented figures only (public repo).

## His answers this plan is built on (tracker #68)
- Shows in the Dashboard to-do from the 1st of the month until he marks it done (he may close the month on day 1–3) (D1, D2).
- Flag thresholds 10% / SAR 200, editable in Settings (D3).
- Suggestions change future months; how many months is flexible, default 12, editable (D4, D8).
- Provisions: review amounts and timing (D5). All patterns (D6).
- Smarter budget = usual monthly spend + annual/irregular items ÷ 12; the app spots annual/irregular items itself, he can mark/unmark them; the ÷ 12 surplus flows to the accrual reserve, so the accrual cap is checked against the annual item (D7).

## What exists today (checked in the code 2026-11-14)
- Expense plan per account per month = its Provisions line if it has one, else its Budget cell (`computeForecastCell`, behaviour `expense`: `provisionsPlanFor` then `budgetPlanFor`). Budget cells are `pd_budget` rows, saved by `saveBudgetCell`.
- Income and other manual accounts plan through `D.forecastPlans` (`getManualPlan` / `setManualPlan`); Smart Rules override some accounts.
- Accrual reserve per sub category: `accruedForCategory`, cap = cap months × that month's budget (`accrualCapForCategory`).
- Per-month history already computed: `historicalStatsFor` (rolling windows, every year).
- Over-budget cover by accrual: `overspendCover`.

## Design
A third tab on **Budget & P&L**: **🗓 Month review** (same page, no new rail button). Three steps on one screen, each collapsible, for the month just closed (picker to review an earlier month).

1. **Actual vs plan (last month).** One row per expense sub category, open to its accounts: plan, actual, difference, %. A row is flagged when it is off by more than both 10% and SAR 200 (R1). Over-budget rows say whether the accrual reserve covered it (`overspendCover`). Income rows shown too, flagged the same way.
2. **Suggested budget (budget-planned accounts only).** For each expense account whose plan comes from Budget cells (not Provisions, not a Smart Rule):
   - *usual* = typical monthly spend over the last 12 complete months, leaving out irregular items (R2);
   - *irregular / annual items* spotted automatically (R3), listed under the account with a ☐ "irregular" tick he can change (remembered);
   - *suggested monthly budget* = usual + irregular total ÷ 12, rounded to SAR 10;
   - a pattern tag with a one-line reason: **rising** (3 months in a row above plan), **falling / under-used** (3 months under), **one-off spike**, **seasonal** (same month last year was high), **new** (under 3 months of history);
   - **Accept** per row (or Accept all ticked) writes the suggested figure into the next N months' Budget cells (N = 12 by default, Settings) after a before/after preview (R4). Nothing changes until he accepts.
   - **Accrual check:** if an account has irregular items and its sub category has an accrual mapping, check that cap months × new budget can hold the largest annual item; if not, suggest the cap months that would.
3. **Provisions roll-forward.** For each provision group, last month's planned lines vs what was actually booked:
   - *amount* differs (by the same thresholds) → offer to set the future months' line to the actual figure;
   - *timing*: the payment landed in a different month than planned → flag it (no automatic change);
   - group whose last planned month is within the next 3 months → offer the existing "copy forward" for it.
   Changes go through the existing provision save path, future months only.

**Finish:** a **Mark month reviewed** button removes the to-do. Undo is possible from the tab.

**Dashboard to-do:** "Review October" from the 1st of the next month until marked reviewed; clicking it opens the tab on that month.

## Settings (Budget & P&L → Month review settings)

## Data
No schema change. Synced in `pd_ui_prefs.dismissals.monthReview` = `{done:{'YYYY-MM':date}, irregular:{ledgerId:true|false}, settings:{pct, sar, months, round}}`. The app never posts JVs; it only writes Budget cells and future provision months he accepts.
