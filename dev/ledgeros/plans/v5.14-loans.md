## Design
1. **New rail page "Loans"** (hideable like other pages). One card per loan: lender, type, start and end, months done / left with a bar, next instalment split (principal / profit / insurance), principal outstanding, profit paid and still to pay, flat rate and APR.
2. **Loan types** (one engine: a list of monthly rows, each split by the effective monthly rate on the balance):
   - *Equal instalments*: amount, term, and any one of flat rate / APR / instalment; the others are solved. Flat rate → total profit = amount × rate × years.
   - *Profit-only period, then instalments*: phase 1 months (profit only, principal unchanged), then the principal steps up to a new figure and equal instalments run for the rest of the term.
   - *Balloon / 50-50*: down payment (not financed), monthly instalments, final payment at the end; insurance as a fixed part of each instalment (not profit, not principal).
   - Entering a loan mid-way: contract terms + first payment date; the app counts months done (every instalment before today treated as paid).
   - Where the bank gives the exact split or instalment, those figures win; the app shows its own rate check next to them.
3. **Schedule table** per loan (collapsible): month, instalment, profit, principal, insurance, balance; paid months grey, this month highlighted; ⬇ CSV.
4. **Early settlement** (SAMA rulebook): pick a month → remaining principal + profit of the next 3 months; shows the amount, profit saved and the payments that disappear. Optional "no settlement before" date (some mortgages). No partial prepayment (his answer B7).
5. **Compare** (light): settle loan X in month M with a new loan Y (amount = settlement, rate, term) → side by side: monthly payment, total still to pay, total profit, end date.
6. **Forecast and JVs through the existing provision** (L3): each loan links to its provision group; "Update provision from schedule" fills that group's **future** months (payable −instalment, liability +principal, profit account +profit, insurance account +insurance) after showing what changes. Past months are never touched. Forecast and the month's provision JV then follow automatically; nothing is posted by the app.
7. **Ledger check** (B9): principal outstanding per the schedule vs the liability account's ledger balance; the difference is shown with a suggested one-time correcting JV for him to post himself.
8. **Data**: new table `pd_loans` (id, name, lender, kind, accounts: liability / profit / payable / insurance, provision_id, contract figures as jsonb, notes, settled_on, updated_at). Additive SQL from `DB_SCHEMA_CORE`; Claude runs it (setup is allowed, 2026-11-13); he adds the table to his backup script. Every table list (load, restore, wipe, sync column map) updated.
